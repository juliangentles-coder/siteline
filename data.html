What this is, and what it isn't

SITELINE captures and labels. It does not rate condition, estimate remaining service life, or express an engineering opinion. Reserve fund studies and building condition assessments in Ontario must be prepared by a qualified person under O. Reg. 48/01 — an engineer, architect, engineering technologist, or accredited appraiser. The imagery above is prepared for that person, not instead of them.
