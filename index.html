Aerial capture for building condition work

for engineers, architects & reserve fund consultants

Reserve fund studies and building condition assessments in Ontario must be prepared by a qualified person under O. Reg. 48/01 — an engineer, architect, engineering technologist, or accredited appraiser. SITELINE is not that, and doesn't pretend to be. What SITELINE does is the part that costs you a lift rental and a day of roof access: getting clean, measurable imagery of the surface so you can do your assessment from your desk.

Roof surface capture

Nadir imagery of the full deck at roughly 2 cm per pixel — membrane surface, ponding marks, flashing, penetrations and edge detail, without swing stage or hatch access.

Labelled asset inventory

Rooftop units, drains, skylight curbs, conduit runs and roof sections located and labelled on the imagery, so your review starts from a marked-up plan rather than a folder of photos.

Facade & elevation runs

Oblique elevation passes for envelope review — sealant lines, staining, spalling and parapet detail, captured at consistent standoff.
